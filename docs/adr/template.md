# ADR-00XX: Title

**Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by ADR-00XX

## Context

What problem forced this decision? What constraints were in play? Keep this
factual — describe the situation, not the solution yet.

## Decision

What did we decide to do, stated as a clear, single sentence if possible.

## Alternatives considered

What else was on the table, and why it was rejected. This is often the most
useful part of the document for a future reader.

## Consequences

What this makes easier, what it makes harder, and what it explicitly gives
up. Include known limitations — a decision with an honestly documented
trade-off is more useful than one that pretends to have no downside.
